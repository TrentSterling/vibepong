VibePong by Tront

VibePong is Pong as a screensaver you cannot stop watching: two CPU players, dynamic physics, powerups, and a rainbow audio-visual treatment over the top. Nobody is playing, which is the point.

Three.js, one page, no install. By Tront (Trent Sterling).
